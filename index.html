Instagram, with an ending.

Story time keeps the best part of Instagram, your friends' stories, and drops the rest.

  • No ads, reels, suggested posts or endless scrolling. Not even reels friends send you.
  • Just stories, from the people you choose, in your own lists: close friends, a hobby, a city.
  • Each list ends with "You're caught up." Open a profile or a message when you mean to.

Set up on iPhone · about 5 minutes

  1. Get the free Userscripts app

    It lets Safari run Story time. Open it once after installing; you don't need to change anything inside.

    Open in the App Store
  2. Turn it on in Safari

    Settings → Apps → Safari → Extensions → Userscripts: switch it on, and set instagram.com (or All Websites) to Allow.

  3. Install Story time

    Open this page in Safari, tap the button, then tap the puzzle-piece (or ᴬA) button in the address bar → Userscripts → Install.

    Install Story time
  4. Open instagram.com in Safari and log in

    Story time greets you and walks you through the rest: bringing in who you follow, making your lists, and adding its icon to your home screen.

    Open instagram.com

It works in Safari, not in the Instagram app, which can't be changed. Once it's set up, move the Instagram app off your home screen so Story time is where you go.

🎬 Reels in Messages: when a friend sends you a reel, you'll see it in the chat, greyed out, but it won't open. Ask them what happens in it.

Everything stays on your phone: your lists, and who you follow. Story time talks only to Instagram, using your own login, and asks for the same things Instagram's story bar does. Updates arrive through the Userscripts app.
