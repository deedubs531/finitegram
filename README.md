# FiniteGram

Instagram, with an ending: stories from the people you choose, without ads, reels or endless scrolling.

**Set up on iPhone:** open https://deedubs531.github.io/finitegram/ in Safari and follow the four steps.

`finitegram.user.js` is a userscript for instagram.com in Safari (via the Userscripts app). Everything
it stores (your lists, who you follow) stays on your phone; it talks only to Instagram, with your own login.
